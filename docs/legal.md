---
title: Legal notes
description: What Integral does and does not do.
---

Integral is a technical tool for issuing and checking licenses. It is **not legal or tax advice** and **not copy protection**.

**Integral does**

- sign licenses with Ed25519 and verify them offline with your public key,
- check Polar license keys through Polar's public API,
- with Integral Pro, issue, renew and revoke licenses from Polar orders and subscriptions.

**Integral does not**

- stop someone who can change your application from removing a license check,
- write your license terms, invoices or privacy notices.

**You decide** what your license terms say, what happens after a cancellation, and which personal data (such as email addresses) you put into licenses and store on your server. Licenses are signed, not encrypted: anyone who has one can read its content.

The free packages `@sweberdev/integral` and `@sweberdev/integral-react` are MIT licensed. Integral Pro has its own license terms, delivered with the packages.
